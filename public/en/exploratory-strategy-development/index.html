Exploratory strategy development

Strategy is like an expedition into the future. Based on a vision focused on customer value, it is important to identify the key drivers of success, regularly review the chosen course, and implement strategic initiatives iteratively and experimentally. However, the decisive factor is a strong leadership team.

On average, 60% of middle management say they are unable to communicate their own company's strategy convincingly. How can you successfully implement something that you haven't fully understood yourself?

The success rate of strategic initiatives increases significantly when those who will later be responsible for implementation are actively involved in strategy development.

At MLI, we bring strategy to life and activate all stakeholders.

We are experts who, as consultants and process facilitators, help you transform your strategy into powerful action. For more than a decade, we have been working on how to apply insights from cognitive research and neuroscience to practical management tasks. With this knowledge, we help you to strategy is not only understood, but also lived out in everyday life.

  • Brain-based mission statement and strategy communication
  • Interactive dialogue and development processes that create clarity and alignment
  • Emotional commitment among managers and teams
  • Accelerated implementation and measurable success

Development of a common goal

Development of an inspiring story about the future of the company that fits the origins, culture and values of the organisation and attention-grabbing visualisations - a strategy map or ‘change on a page’ document. Effective transmission of content and future skills

Cascading workshops and reflection formats to clarify the individual contribution to overall success at all levels and to practice new ways of thinking and behaving.

The digital strategy workshop for your management team

At MLI, we want to empower your leadership team to develop smart strategies and put them into practice.

That's why we offer our customers access to the digital strategy workshop. Here, your managers have access to relevant knowledge at any time. They can decide for themselves when and where to use the more than 60 targeted tutorials, strategy tools, and worksheets for their own strategy work.

An experienced MLI coach will work with your management team or individual managers to develop a customized approach to solving strategic challenges and putting them into practice.

MLI Digital Strategy Workshop

  • 9 digital workspaces for specific aspects of strategy work (from strategic analysis to implementation using OKR)
  • >60 video tutorials with specific strategy tools and approaches
  • Reflection and worksheets for solving strategic issues in a structured manner
  • Individual work plan, depending on the strategic task
  • Accompanying personal coaching sessions with an experienced MLI coach

Benefits:

  • Valuable content, explained in a practical way
  • Available and ready to use immediately
  • Scalable: from 1 to 250 users
  • Cost-effective alternative to external strategy consulting
