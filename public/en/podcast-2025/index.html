New Leadership Podcast – Guests who are redefining leadership


In recent years, we have had the opportunity to learn from outstanding personalities. Among them were Wikipedia co-founder Jimmy Wales, Nobel Peace Prize winner Muhammad Yunus, Bosch CEO Volkmar Denner, Miss Germany 2023 Kira Geiss, Harvard professor Barbara Kellerman, economic philosopher Nassim Nicholas Taleb, Flixbus co-founder Jochen Engert, psychologist Barry Schwartz, long-time Siemens CEO Joe Kaeser and many others.



Your host: Sebastian Morgner


Sebastian Morgner is co-founder of the Future of Leadership Initiative, managing partner of the MLI Leadership Institute, author and strategy coach. For over twenty years, he has been supporting management teams in strategy activation and transformation processes. As a strategy coach, he assists executives in decision-making.

In its cover story ‘You can do better’ (issue 12 on coaching, March 2021), Wirtschaftswoche magazine wrote about him: ‘It is his frankness that distinguishes a coach like Morgner from a manager and can stimulate fruitful exchange between the two.’

Er ist Autor des Buches „Expedition Zukunft: Wie wir den Zufall nutzen können, um Ungewissheit und Komplexität zu meistern“. Dieses Buch richtet sich an Führungskräfte und junge Talente, die ihre Führungsrolle wirkungsvoll nutzen möchten. Basierend auf Erkenntnissen aus der Neurowissenschaft hat das MLI Leadership Institut ein Führungskonzept entwickelt, das Mitarbeitende mit unterschiedlichsten Haltungen zu einer produktiven Gemeinschaft vereint: Für eine erfolgreiche Expedition in Richtung Zukunft.

Er blickt auf eine Karriere in der Finanzbranche zurück, war vor seiner Selbstständigkeit Marketingvorstand bei einer europäischen Großbank, ist ausgebildeter Coach, Mentaltrainer und Speaker. Sebastian Morgner lebt mit seiner Frau, drei Töchtern und Hund Fritz am Ammersee in der Nähe von München.


FAQ New Leadership Podcast

What is the New Leadership Podcast?

The New Leadership Podcast is a series of conversations hosted by Sebastian Morgner. Here, top decision-makers and thought leaders discuss current leadership topics, trends and strategies. Learn how leading personalities motivate people, steer organisations and achieve desirable goals.

Who is the New Leadership Podcast suitable for?

The New Leadership Podcast is aimed at executives, young talents and anyone interested in modern leadership topics. Whether you already hold a leadership position or are preparing for such a role, this podcast offers valuable insights and inspiration.

Where can I listen to the New Leadership Podcast?

You can listen to the New Leadership Podcast on all popular platforms such as Spotify, Apple Podcasts and Amazon Podcasts. You will find links to the respective streaming services on this page.

Who are the guests on the New Leadership Podcast?

The New Leadership Podcast features influential CEOs and dedicated employees. Previous guests have included Wikipedia co-founder Jimmy Wales, Miss Germany 2023 Kira Geiss, Nobel Peace Prize winner Muhammad Yunus and Bosch CEO Volkmar Denner.

How can I contact the New Leadership Podcast team?

If you have any questions or suggestions about the podcast, or would like to arrange an appointment for a podcast recording, you can contact us using the contact form on our website. We look forward to hearing from you!
