Strong in Leadership –
Leadership Development for modern Companies



Young talents increasingly judge the attractiveness of an employer based on its management culture and the quality of its managers. You are probably familiar with the saying: “People apply for an exciting job – and quit because of their manager.”

In fact, most companies do not have reliable standards that are applied in everyday life for how people exercise their leadership roles: how are goals agreed upon, tasks delegated, feedback given, and what happens in a development meeting?

Do you want to offer your managers access to scientifically sound and practical management expertise at the touch of a button? And do you want your decision-makers to transfer this knowledge as effectively as possible into their own day-to-day management activities?

The “Strong in Leadership” program combines a digital leadership academy with more than 60 targeted tutorials and worksheets with individual support.

An experienced MLI coach will work with your management team or individual managers to put together a customized learning journey designed to quickly resolve acute challenges in your own management context.

If you would like to use the MLI knowledge base to establish a digital leadership academy in your own CD with your own initiators, we would be happy to implement such a project with you.

MLI Leadership “Strong in Leadership” program

Program:

  • 9 digital learning spaces on contemporary leadership topics
  • More than 60 video tutorials with leadership knowledge and inspiration
  • Reflection and worksheets for working on specific management tasks
  • Individual leadership journey based on the current situation and challenges faced by managers
  • Accompanying personal leadership coaching sessions with an experienced MLI coach.

Advantages:

  • Valuable content, explained in a practical way
  • Available and ready to use immediately
  • Scalable: from 1 to 1,000+ users
  • Cost-effective alternative to a self-developed leadership development program
  • Constant updating.

Learn in just 30 minutes how the program
“Strong in Leadership”
can help your company quickly and effectively introduce common leadership standards and empower your managers?
